# Grant budget and budget narrative

The budget is the proposal written in numbers. Reviewers check that every cost is necessary for the activities,
reasonable, calculated transparently, allowable under the funder's rules, and consistent with the narrative. This is a
drafting aid: the organisation's finance lead must review and approve the final budget and any indirect rate claim.

## 1. Inputs

- Funder's budget template, categories, cost rules, cap on indirect costs, match/cost-share requirement, and allowed
  period (12/24/36 months; per-year breakdown needed?).
- Activities, staffing plan and timeline from the proposal and logic model.
- Organisation salary scale, fringe rate (actual benefits % of salary), and negotiated indirect cost rate agreement
  (NICRA) if any.
- Quotes for equipment, contracts and significant purchases.

## 2. Build the budget

Standard categories (US federal SF-424A uses these; most foundations accept similar):

| Category | How to calculate |
| --- | --- |
| Personnel | Name/position × annual salary × % FTE (or hours × rate) × months/12 |
| Fringe benefits | Personnel × fringe rate (state the rate and what it includes) |
| Travel | Trips × travellers × (airfare + lodging × nights + per diem × days); use GSA rates for US federal |
| Equipment | Items meeting the funder's equipment threshold (federal: unit cost ≥ $10,000 and useful life > 1 year under the 2024 Uniform Guidance revision, or the organisation's lower policy) |
| Supplies | Items below the equipment threshold, itemised by type |
| Contractual | Subawards and contracts; attach scope and cost basis for each |
| Other | Participant support, rent, printing, evaluation, etc. |
| Indirect | Base × rate (see below) |

Show the formula for each line, not just the total. Round consistently (whole dollars). Apply annual cost-of-living
increases (state the %) in multi-year budgets.

## 3. Indirect costs

- Use the funder's rule first: many foundations cap overhead (e.g. 10–15%) or pay none; some accept full cost.
- US federal awards: use the organisation's NICRA if it has one. Without one, organisations may elect the de minimis
  rate, which the 2024 Uniform Guidance revision raised to **up to 15% of modified total direct costs (MTDC)**.
- MTDC excludes equipment, rent, participant support costs, tuition remission, and the portion of each subaward above
  $50,000 (2024 revision). Calculate the base explicitly.
- Don't double-count: costs charged as indirect (e.g. finance staff, general rent) can't also appear as direct lines.

## 4. Match / cost share

If required, show cash and in-kind separately, with source and valuation method (volunteer hours at a documented rate,
donated space at fair market rent). Only commit match the organisation has confirmed; label pending sources.

## 5. Budget narrative (justification)

For each line: **what** it is, **why** it's needed (tie to a named activity or outcome), and **how** it was calculated.

```text
Program Coordinator (1.0 FTE, 12 months): $52,000. Recruits and enrols 120 families, schedules all
workshops and maintains the participant database used for outcome tracking (Activities 1–3).
Salary is based on the organisation's FY26 pay scale. $52,000 × 100% × 12/12 = $52,000.

Fringe benefits at 24% of salary: $12,480. Covers FICA (7.65%), health insurance, workers'
compensation and unemployment insurance per the organisation's actual FY25 rates.
```

Follow the template order exactly; use the same names for positions as the staffing section.

## 6. Consistency check (run before handing over)

- [ ] Totals sum correctly per category, per year and overall; the requested amount matches the cover sheet and
      narrative everywhere it appears.
- [ ] Every activity in the narrative is resourced; every budget line maps to an activity.
- [ ] FTEs match the staffing plan; no person is charged > 100% across all funding sources.
- [ ] Indirect base and rate follow funder rules; cap respected.
- [ ] Unallowable costs removed (US federal typical: alcohol, entertainment, lobbying, fundraising, bad debt —
      verify against 2 CFR 200 Subpart E and the specific program notice).
- [ ] Match is documented and confirmed.
- [ ] Assumptions (rates, quotes, COLA) are stated.

Use a short script or spreadsheet formulas to recompute totals rather than adding by hand; report any discrepancy.

## Pitfalls

- Lump sums ("Program costs: $40,000") with no breakdown.
- Inflating lines to absorb unfunded overhead — reviewers spot it; request indirect honestly.
- Budget built before the program design, so narrative and numbers drift apart.
- Forgetting that federal funds require cost allocation documentation (timesheets for split-funded staff).
